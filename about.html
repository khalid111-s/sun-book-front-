Our Story

Welcome to The Sun Book, a sanctuary for seekers of truth and ancient wisdom. We traverse the boundaries of time to bring you the most profound esoteric literature, hidden knowledge, and forgotten rituals.

The Sun Book is an Egyptian online bookstore based in El Agamy, Alexandria, Egypt. We offer real physical books with clear prices in Egyptian Pounds (EGP), digital books that can be read online, and private one-on-one sessions. We deliver physical orders to every governorate in Egypt and serve digital readers and session guests worldwide.

Our Mission

For centuries, the wisdom of the ancients has been guarded by secret societies and lost in the sands of time. Our mission is to curate and preserve these mystical texts, making them accessible to modern scholars, occultists, and those who dare to look beyond the veil.
