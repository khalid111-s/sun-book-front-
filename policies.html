Privacy Policy

At The Sun Book, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

Information we collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number when you create an account or place an order.
  • Your delivery address and order details for physical book orders.
  • Payment status and transaction reference. We do not store your full card details; card payments are handled by our secure payment gateway.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited and approximate location, used to improve the website and prevent fraud.
  • Cookies and local storage needed to keep your cart, language preference and login session working.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders, digital books and one-on-one sessions.
  • To contact you about your order, delivery or customer support request.
  • To prevent fraud, secure our website and comply with Egyptian legal requirements.
  • To improve our products, website experience and customer service.
  • To send order-related updates. Marketing emails are only sent when you subscribe, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing your information

We never sell, trade or rent your personal information. We only share the minimum data required with trusted service providers such as delivery couriers, payment processors, hosting providers and analytics services. These providers are required to protect your data and use it only for the service they provide to us.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep your shopping cart, remember your language and login session, and understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the website — including checkout — may not work correctly.

Data security and retention

We use HTTPS encryption, secure servers, access controls and industry-standard safeguards to protect your data. We keep personal data only for as long as your account is active or as required for legal, tax and accounting purposes.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. You can also delete your account from your Profile page. To exercise any of these rights, email us at thesunbook7@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Children

Our website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with its effective date.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. The terms below explain when you can cancel an order, return an item, or request a refund.

Physical books — cancellation before shipping

You may cancel a physical book order for a full refund at any time before the order is shipped. Contact us by email or WhatsApp with your order number and we will cancel it and arrange the refund.

Physical books — returns after delivery

  • You may request a return within 14 days of delivery.
  • Books must be unused, undamaged and in their original condition and packaging.
  • Please contact us before returning any item so we can provide the correct return instructions.
  • Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility, unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.
  • If your book arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund.

Digital books

Digital books are delivered instantly to your account and can be read online immediately after payment. Because access is granted immediately, digital books are non-refundable and non-cancellable once accessed, except where there is a duplicate charge or a technical fault that we cannot resolve. If you experience a technical problem, contact us and we will do our best to fix it.

One-on-one sessions

You may reschedule or cancel a session for a full refund up to 4 hours before the scheduled start time from the "Upcoming Sessions" section in your Profile. Cancellations made less than 4 hours before the session, or missed sessions, are not automatically refundable. If something unexpected happens, contact us and we will review your case fairly.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method whenever possible. If you paid cash on delivery, our team will contact you to arrange the refund. Refunds are usually completed within 7–14 business days after approval, depending on your bank or payment provider. Delivery fees, if any, are refunded only when the return is due to our error.

How to request a refund or cancellation

Email thesunbook7@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +20 128 760 7124. Please include your order number, the item concerned and a short description of the issue. We aim to reply within one business day.

Delivery & Shipping Policy

The Sun Book delivers physical books to every governorate in Egypt. Digital books and one-on-one sessions are delivered online and are available worldwide.

Physical book delivery inside Egypt

  • Shipping cost: Free delivery on all physical book orders inside Egypt. The price shown at checkout is the final price — no extra delivery fees.
  • Order processing: Orders are prepared within 1–2 business days after confirmation. Orders placed on Friday, Saturday or public holidays are processed on the next business day.
  • Delivery time: Cairo and Alexandria usually receive orders within 2–5 business days. Other governorates usually receive orders within 3–7 business days. Remote areas may take up to 10 business days.
  • Courier contact: Our courier will call or message you on the phone number provided at checkout to arrange delivery.
  • Tracking: You can follow your order status from your Profile under "Physical Orders & Tracking" (Processing, Shipped or Delivered).

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for physical book orders across Egypt. You pay the courier in cash when your order arrives. Please make sure the phone number and address you provide are accurate and that someone is available to receive the order.

Digital books

Digital books are delivered instantly to your account after successful payment and appear in "My Digital Library" in your Profile. There is no shipping charge, and digital books can be read online from anywhere in the world. An internet connection is required to read them.

One-on-one sessions

After payment, your session is confirmed and appears under "Upcoming Sessions" in your Profile with a "Join" button that activates at the scheduled time. Sessions are held online, so no shipping is required.

International orders

Physical book shipping is currently available inside Egypt only. Customers outside Egypt can still purchase digital books and book online sessions, which are delivered worldwide.

Failed or delayed delivery

Our courier will usually attempt delivery twice. If you miss both attempts, the order may be returned to us and we will contact you to arrange re-delivery. If your order is delayed, please contact us with your order number and we will follow it up with the courier. You can change your delivery address before the order ships by contacting us as soon as possible.
